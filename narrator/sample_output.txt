Mamaearth Returns & Growth Intelligence – Business Narrative

1. Revenue Overview
The cleaned total revenue is ₹97,358.3, compared with raw revenue of ₹99,860.2.
The difference of ₹2,501.9 was identified during duplicate-order reconciliation.

2. Return Risk by Payment Method
COD has the highest return rate at 44.4%.
CARD has a return rate of 14.7%, while UPI has a return rate of 18.9%.

3. Highest-Risk Customer Segment
The highest-risk segment is customers using COD in City Tier 2,
with a return rate of 54.5%.

4. Revenue Peak
The true peak revenue month was 2026-03, with revenue of ₹20,318.9.

5. Business Recommendation
The business should focus on reducing COD-related returns,
particularly within Tier 2 cities. Duplicate-order controls and
regular data-quality checks should also be maintained to ensure
accurate revenue reporting.
